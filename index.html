The simulation is a two-dimensional lattice Boltzmann flow solver. Velocity, length and viscosity are in lattice (simulation) units, not m/s, metres or m²/s; only their combination, the Reynolds number, matters for what the flow looks like. For every model the characteristic length D is the width facing the flow (20 mm on the printed models), so Reynolds numbers compare like for like. The teardrop uses the profile from the CAD drawing, scaled to the chosen width and length. For the aerofoil, D is the chord, the usual choice for aerofoil Reynolds numbers, and it rotates about its quarter-chord point.
